# Meaningful Plug-in Theater

An experimental record of external-tool performances in which a plugin earns its role by changing what can be known or done.

## Rule

A successful invocation is not evidence of usefulness. A plugin must produce a useful observation, verification, artifact, authorized state change, measurable response, or enabling capability.

Consequential mutations are not considered complete until external state is observed after the attempted change.
